These are Animals

  1. Lion

    The lion is a wild animal known as the king of the jungle. It has a strong body, golden fur, and a long tail with a tuft at the end. The male lion has a thick mane around its neck, which makes it look very majestic. Lions live in groups called prides. They are found mainly in Africa and India. Lions are carnivores, which means they eat the flesh of other They are brave, powerful, and one of the most respected animals in the world.

  2. Dog

    The dog is a faithful and friendly animal. It is one of the most common pets kept by people all over the world. Dogs come in different sizes, colors, and breeds. They are very loyal to their owners and can protect their homes from strangers. Dogs have a strong sense of smell and hearing, which makes them useful for police and rescue They love to play and are known as man’s best friend because of their love and loyalty.

  3. cow

    The cow is a domestic animal. It is a gentle and useful creature. The cow gives us milk, which is very healthy and full of nutrients. Milk is used to make butter, curd, cheese, and many other dairy. Cows are found in almost every village and city. They eat grass and fodder. The cow is respected and often called a holy animal in India. It is a kind and helpful animal that provides many benefits to people..
